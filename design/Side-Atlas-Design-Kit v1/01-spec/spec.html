What this is

The first complete visual language for Side Atlas, the ledger for global side income with a tax layer on top. Everything here — tokens, components, every desktop screen — is decided at once, in one version. The clickable prototype is 07-mockups/app.html; the screen images in 08-screens/desktop are exported from it with real numbers from the fictional test set.

Reference. Linktree, for form: big rounded cards, pill buttons, heavy rounded headline type, one bold colour field per screen. Not for colour. Side Atlas has its own palette built around a deep forest green (the user's choice).

The brand test. A screen passes if it reads like a creator's own money notebook — one confident forest-green field, calm white tables, every figure dark and tabular — and fails if any number sits on a bright colour or the page looks like a sticker sheet, even when every token matches.
PrincipleRules
One bold field per screenExactly one .sa-hero, always forest. Everything else is white, sage or a light tint.
Money is calmMoney figures are ink or forest on white/sage, tabular, right-aligned in tables. A deduction may use its semantic text colour. The only figure on a colour field is the hero figure: white, ≥48px, on forest.
Lime is a shape, not a text colourLime only for large shapes, bars, dots and the countdown numeral (≥48px). Never small text on lime; never small lime text.
Fees and tax never share a colourPlatform fee = clay, payout fee = stone, FX = slate, tax withheld = tax blue, net = forest.
Stop, don't guessMissing data shows a light warning card beside the forest hero, names the record and offers one action. Totals that skip a record are marked incomplete and say what they leave out.
Pills and big radiiButtons, chips and segmented controls are full pills. Cards 24px, inner panels 16px, inputs 14px, badges 8px.

6 · Mobile

Out of scope for v1 (desktop demo). Layouts must not break below 1280px; a phone layout is a later version.

7 · Notifications

Two levels only. Stop (light warning card + nav count badge; v1.1): missing exchange rate or any input the math needs; the run is marked failed in PRISM and the alert fires. Note (sage or warn-tint inline note): threshold and duplicate-report information, import results. No toasts.

Release checks

  • Each screen has exactly one hero colour field (count .sa-hero = 1).
  • No text smaller than 48px sits on lime; no lime text below 48px.
  • Every money figure uses tabular figures (font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums) and money columns are right-aligned.
  • Tax withheld never uses the fee colour; fee columns are clay, withheld is tax blue.
  • Every non-USD ledger row shows its rate and the rate's date.
  • A missing rate shows the light warning card (hero stays forest, marked incomplete), names the record and leaves it out of totals — no estimated value anywhere on the page.
  • Colours in pages come only from --sa-* tokens (no hex in markup or component CSS beyond the token file).
  • Set-aside copy says the rate was chosen by the person; the disclaimer is visible on every screen.
  • All ten 08-screens match the built pages side by side (ui-diff compare) with no clipped column at 1440×900.
  • Only Plus Jakarta Sans (local woff2) and the CJK system font load; no CDN fonts.
  • v1.3 · In Your data mode, no request to the server, Ask Atlas or PRISM carries a name, an account ID, an order number or a file (check the network log); accounts appear only as acct ••NNNN.
  • v1.3 · Delete all my data leaves this site's localStorage and IndexedDB empty and returns to screen 10.
  • v1.3 · The currency switch changes display only; switching back to USD shows the same figures as before.
  • v1.3.1 · Every converted amount in a CNY view carries a rate line with the rate, its date and its source; no converted figure appears without one.
  • v1.3.1 · No screen states an amount of tax owed, in either country.
  • Icons render from the sprite with currentColor; no emoji in the UI.
