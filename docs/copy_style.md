# Side Pocket copy style (for GPT-6 Astra briefs)

All product and marketing copy is written by GPT-6 Astra (`scripts/astra.py`). Paste this guide into
every Astra brief. Decided 2026-10-17 after the founder rejected lines like "Your tax items, together.",
"Your forms and bank deposits, side by side" and "to set aside: $5,428.94 net × the 25% you chose" as
AI-sounding.

- Names: the product is **Side Pocket**; the pigeon and the assistant are **Tilly** ("Ask Tilly").
  The sample account is **Moon Spirit**, presented as the person using the app (no "demo" wording in the UI).
- Write like a real, well-made finance product (Mercury, Monzo, Wise, Stripe dashboards).
- Headings and labels: 1 to 4 words, nouns, sentence case. Say what is there, not a slogan.
- No taglines, no "together", "side by side", "ready for", "one place", "at a glance", no rhetorical
  rhythm, no lists of three, no hype, no exclamation marks, no em dashes or en dashes.
- Do not start most lines with "Your".
- Helper text: one short plain sentence (under about 14 words), or nothing if it repeats the heading
  or the numbers on screen.
- Keep every fact exact; never add claims. Keep placeholders such as {month} or {rate} as written.
- Product UI is English only; no bilingual labels.
